# Species coverage lists

Two CSVs derived from the 708-window offline soundscape bank, generated by
`src/part2_perch_heads/export_species_lists.py`:

- **`covered_species_71.csv`** — the **71 covered species**: target species with **>= 1 positive
  label** in the 708-window labelled soundscape bank. These are the species the offline
  out-of-fold (OOF) macro-AUC endpoint can actually score.
- **`absent_species_163.csv`** — the **163 absent species**: target species with **0 positives**
  in that bank. The offline endpoint cannot evaluate them; this coverage gap drives the paper's
  main finding (a head can improve the covered-species offline benchmark while damaging the
  absent species in the full system).

71 + 163 = **234** target species.

## Columns
- `species_index` — 0-based position in the competition submission column order.
- `species_code` — eBird/iNat code used by the competition.
- `taxon` — `class_name` from `taxonomy.csv` (Aves / Amphibia / Insecta / Mammalia / Reptilia).
- `n_positive_windows` — number of the 708 windows labelled positive for this species
  (>= 1 for covered, 0 for absent).
- `perch_logit_mapped` — `True` if the species has a Perch logit (direct **or** taxonomic proxy).
- `perch_match_corr` — embedding-match correlation of that mapping: `~1.0` = direct mapping,
  `<1.0` = taxonomic proxy (e.g. 0.75), `0.0` = no Perch logit.

## Offline-bank coverage is NOT the same as Perch coverage
Two distinct notions:
- **Offline-bank coverage** (this file): does the species have a positive label in the 708-window
  soundscape bank? → **71 covered / 163 absent**.
- **Perch logit coverage** (`perch_logit_mapped`): does Perch produce a logit for the species?
  → **206 mapped / 28 unmapped**. Of the 206 mapped, the paper reports **203 direct + 3 proxy**;
  a `>0.99` correlation cut on this artifact yields 204 direct / 2 proxy (a one-species boundary
  difference — both agree on 206 mapped and 28 unmapped). The two proxy species here have
  `perch_match_corr` ≈ 0.75.

A species can be Perch-mapped yet absent from the offline bank (most of the 163), or present in
the offline bank yet Perch-unmapped (e.g. the single Reptilia species `116570`, which has 13
positive windows but `perch_match_corr = 0.0`).
